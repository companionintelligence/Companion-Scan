block below is the probe's own report from logcat (tag ScanXrProbe), unedited; the last section is read from run 2's
recording.

  camera does not open and counts the tracking states): CAMERA and HEAD_TRACKING granted with pm grant at the owner's

## From run 2's recording

Read from its probe.json, poses.csv, frames.csv and keyframes.jsonl. The JPEGs were not looked at: they show a room.
The recording stays on the device, in the app's own folder.

- Render viewpoints (the displays), relative to the device pose: left (-0.0315, 0, 0) m, right (+0.0315, 0, 0) m, no
  rotation, so the device pose sits midway between the eyes. Field of view per eye about 29.5° left and right, 18.3°
  up and 18.1° down. There is no mono viewpoint (the device renders in stereo).
- Head pose: 646 updates, all TRACKING, 21.2 to 32.0 ms apart (median 23.2 ms, 43 Hz). The mark's age when handled:
  21.2 to 32.2 ms (median 23.4). The bracket round the age read: median 29 us, at most 223 us.
- At rest the pose stayed within 2.7, 1.0 and 1.4 mm (x, y, z) and 0.34° of its first value. Its largest step between
  updates: 0.21 mm. Epoch bumps: none.
- Camera 0: 434 frames, 26.7 to 40.0 ms apart (median 33.33 ms, 30 Hz). Exposure 10.0 to 16.7 ms (median 16.7).
  Rolling shutter skew 13.7 ms on every frame. The readout started one exposure after the frame's timestamp on every
  frame, so the timestamp is the start of exposure, as Camera2 defines it. The middle of a frame's exposure, at its
  middle row, is therefore about 15 ms after its timestamp.
- A frame reached the app 90 to 139 ms after its timestamp (median 105), on the boot clock: consistent with REALTIME.
- Every one of the 434 frames had tracked poses either side within 29 ms, so each has a pose in frames.csv.
- 28 keyframes, every 500 ms: 1920 x 1080 JPEGs whose sizes match their keyframes.jsonl lines. Each line has the keys
  index, timestamp, capturedAt, pose, epoch, intrinsics, file, bytes and receivedAt. The intrinsics are the estimate
  (fx = fy = 1440, cx 960, cy 540: 67.4° by 41.1°).
